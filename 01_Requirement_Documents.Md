# Hospital Management & Patient Analytics System

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to design a relational database
for managing hospital operations and analyzing hospital data.

The system will manage:
- Patient information
- Doctor information
- Hospital departments
- Patient appointments
- Prescriptions
- Hospital rooms
- Patient admissions
- Billing information

## 2. Business Requirements

The hospital should be able to:

1. Store and manage patient information.
2. Store doctor and specialization details.
3. Manage hospital departments.
4. Schedule and track patient appointments.
5. Store prescriptions associated with appointments.
6. Manage hospital rooms and their availability.
7. Track patient admissions and discharges.
8. Generate billing records for admissions.
9. Analyze doctor performance.
10. Analyze patient visits and hospital revenue.

## 3. Database Entities

The database will contain the following entities:

1. Patients
2. Doctors
3. Departments
4. Appointments
5. Prescriptions
6. Rooms
7. Admissions
8. Billing

## 4. Expected Analysis

The database should help answer questions such as:

- Which doctor has the highest number of appointments?
- Which department receives the most patients?
- What is the total hospital revenue?
- Which doctors generate the highest consultation revenue?
- What is the average billing amount?
- How many patients are currently admitted?
- Which room types are used most frequently?
- What are the most common diagnoses?
- What is the monthly appointment trend?
